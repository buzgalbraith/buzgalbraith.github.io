**Authors:** Harshit Soni, Gabriel Nixon, Woodward Galbraith, Benjamin M. Gyori
**Presented at:** ACM BCB 2026 (poster, abstract)

A weakly supervised, retrieval-augmented approach for identifying equivalent classes across biomedical ontologies.

* Developed a weakly supervised RAG approach for identifying equivalent classes across ontologies, using a SapBERT embedding model fine-tuned on SeMRA ontology-mapping landscapes.
* The model improves hits@1 over a TF-IDF baseline on the HP–MP ontology-matching benchmark.
