**Authors:** Woodward Galbraith, Xiudi Li, Benjamin M. Gyori, Larry Han
**Presented at:** STAI-X 2026 (poster, abstract)

A general-purpose method, built on the conformal-inference framework, that achieves statistical risk control when linking plain text to standard resource identifiers.

* Developed a general-purpose method leveraging the conformal-inference framework to achieve statistical risk control for linking plain text to standard resource identifiers.
* The method achieves a 50% reduction in candidate set size while only reducing hits@5 from 0.75 to 0.73 for the KRISSBERT model on the BC5DR benchmark dataset.
