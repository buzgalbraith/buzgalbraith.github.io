Work from my time in the Cirrone Lab (NYU Langone Health). I built a prediction model for individual patient response to biologic DMARDs using longitudinal data from 600+ rheumatoid arthritis patients, developing a novel two-stage ensemble model (severity regression followed by response classification) that improved classification accuracy from 0.67 to 0.75 over baseline while increasing clinical interpretability.
