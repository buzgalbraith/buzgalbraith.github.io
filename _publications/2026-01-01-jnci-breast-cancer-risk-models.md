While at the Kaiser Permanente Division of Research, I contributed data curation and formal analysis to this comparative study of AI, polygenic, and clinical breast cancer risk models. My work included optimizing and containerizing DICOM-based breast cancer risk models (Mirai) and ensuring continuous model availability across 250,000+ patient records via GitLab CI/CD.
