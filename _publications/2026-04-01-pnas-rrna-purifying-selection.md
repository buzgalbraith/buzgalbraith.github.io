As part of the Hochwagen Lab (NYU Department of Biology), I contributed to this study of purifying selection on the human 47S ribosomal RNA genes. I built a pipeline to download, filter, and process rRNA sequences from large public databases, assembling a dataset of 3,202+ humans' rRNA across diverse populations, and analyzed sequence variance to identify highly conserved regions where mutations are likely to affect health.
