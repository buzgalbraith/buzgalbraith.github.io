Work co-mentored with a group of NYU M.S. in Data Science students for their program capstone project. We developed a weakly supervised RAG approach for identifying equivalent classes across ontologies, leveraging a SapBERT embedding model fine-tuned on SeMRA ontology mapping landscapes. The method improves hits@1 from 0.79 to 0.93 over a TF-IDF baseline on the MONDO–MeSH ontology matching benchmark. See the [project page](/portfolio/cross-ontology-mapping/) for more detail.
